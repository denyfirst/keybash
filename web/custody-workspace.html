Checking your account…

Protect your sign-in with your phone as well. Keys and passwords stay locked until this is done. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open an authenticator app on your phone

    Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Aegis or 2FAS all work.

  2. Scan the QR code

    Add a new account in the app and point the camera at the code.

    A password alone is not enough: this code makes sure only you set up this account's first factor.

    Can't scan? Enter the key by hand

    Choose "enter a setup key" in the app. Account: Keybash, type: time-based (TOTP).

    This QR code is your account's secret key: don't photograph or share it. It hides when you switch tabs.

  3. Enter the 6-digit code from the app

    The code changes every 30 seconds. Enter the current one.

Enter the code from your phone

Open your authenticator app and enter the 6-digit code it shows for Keybash.

Phone not at hand? Enter one of your recovery codes instead.

Password

The password you sign in to Keybash with. Changing it closes all open sessions.

Authenticator

The 6-digit code on your phone. Replace it when you change phones: the old one keeps working until the new one is confirmed.

Recovery codes

One-time codes for signing in when your phone is lost. Making new ones stops the old ones at once.

Add a key or password

What you add is encrypted before it is saved.

Usually named id_ed25519 or id_rsa. Don’t pick the one ending in .pub: that is the public half.

Keybash makes a new SSH key. Its private half stays encrypted here and is never shown; you add only its public half to the server.

Change password

All open sessions close, and you sign in again with the new password.

At least 15 characters; choose a long, unique one.
